Welcome! I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Economics at Queen's University. My research interests are in Empirical Industrial Organization and Competition Policy.  

My current research focuses on understanding how compatibility among tied-goods products affects consumer demand and testing the efficacy of screens for bid-rigging as well applications to regulated baby formula auctions in the US WIC program.  

I'm currently on the job market and available for interviews.
